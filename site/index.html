Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding features, privacy, customization, and releases.

Speed & Efficiency How fast is T9 contact search?
+

Rivo uses an optimized in-memory trie algorithm that matches both names and phone numbers in sub-millisecond response times. Even libraries with 10,000+ contacts resolve with zero UI stutter or dropped frames.

Material 3 What are Material 3 Expressive shapes & atmospheric blur?
+

Rivo is built with the latest Material 3 Expressive design tokens. Choose from 36 distinctive avatar shapes—including our signature 9-sided Cookie (COOKIE_9), Scalloped, Flower, Star, Pentagon, and Rounded Polygon geometries. In-call and incoming screens feature real-time Gaussian background blurs derived from the contact's photo and your dynamic Android wallpaper palette.

Security How does the Biometric Private Vault protect sensitive contacts?
+

Contacts assigned to your Private Vault are strictly sequestered from Android's shared contacts provider. Their call logs and frequency records are kept in an encrypted, isolated database, and their incoming call notifications can be masked or hidden until unlocked with your fingerprint, face authentication, or device PIN.

Channels What is the difference between Google Play and GitHub Preview Release?
+

The Google Play release is our thoroughly tested, stable production channel with seamless background auto-updates and support for the in-app Tip Jar. The Preview / Beta channel (published on our public GitHub repository) provides immediate access to bleeding-edge features, new experimental UI animations, and rapid test builds before they reach public release.

Dual SIM Does Rivo support Dual SIM devices and speed dialing?
+

Yes! Rivo includes intelligent Dual SIM support. You can set preferred SIM cards per contact, switch SIMs on the fly directly from the dialpad with a single tap, and view clear carrier badges on all call logs. Long-pressing any digit 1–9 triggers instant speed dial to your configured favorite contacts.

Zero Telemetry Is Rivo truly privacy-first? Does it track or upload any data?
+

Rivo operates under a strict zero-telemetry policy. All contact indexing, T9 lookup calculations, and call analytics remain 100% on your local device. Rivo has no remote telemetry servers, requires no user account, serves zero advertisements, and never sells or uploads your personal information.
